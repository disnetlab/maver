The packet's `external_feedback` is an external evaluation of this session — a source of truth. Treat its diagnosis/mitigation verdicts as ground truth: where the session's own artefacts or self-assessment disagree with it, believe the external evaluation. Scores-only feedback can confirm or reject the submitted hypothesis/action but does not reveal the corrected cause: leave `corrected_cause` empty in that case. When `ground_truth_root_cause` is present, copy it as the correction when the submitted diagnosis was rejected. Never copy external feedback or its ground truth into `observations`; those belong in `corrected_cause` and the audit summary. Do not carry forward reasoning the evaluation contradicts.
