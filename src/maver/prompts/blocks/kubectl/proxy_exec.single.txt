- `bash_exec` / `code_exec`: run normal `kubectl` commands directly (read and mutating verbs). They resolve to a stage-aware shim that routes through the SREGym MCP filtering proxy. `exec_kubectl_cmd_safely` and the rollback tools are not available in this mode. Do not use `kubectl wait` or `kubectl rollout status`; verify with short `kubectl get ... -o json/jsonpath` reads, Ready replicas, pods, events, logs, endpoints, or the SLI. Never use interactive or attaching flags (`-i`, `-t`, `-it`) — they wedge the proxy.
